FFmpeg 9.0.2, audio-only LGPL build for Windows x64

Libraries: Fraunhofer FDK AAC 2.0.3, LAME 3.100, Opus 1.5.2, libogg 1.3.6, libvorbis 1.3.7, zlib 1.3.2
FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
FDK AAC source: https://downloads.sourceforge.net/opencore-amr/fdk-aac-2.0.3.tar.gz
Build scripts: https://github.com/DerekYang2/ffmpeg-audio
